CELPIP Templates · Writing Task 1

The Email

27 minutes · 150–200 words
Elevate with Ms. Maria · Learnovate Qatar
Your 27 minutes
3 min plan · 20 min write · 4 min proofread
The golden rule
One paragraph for each bullet, in the same order as the prompt.
Check the reader
Manager / company / landlord → formal
Neighbour / colleague → semi-formal
Friend / family → friendly

Template · Formal email

Dear Mr./Ms. / Dear Customer Service Manager,

Purpose I am writing to (complain about / request / inform you about) .

Bullet 1 On , I . Unfortunately, .

Bullet 2 As a result, . This has been very (inconvenient / stressful / costly) because .

Bullet 3 I would appreciate it if you could . Alternatively, would also be acceptable.

Close Thank you for your attention to this matter. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Sincerely,
(your full name)

I hope you’re doing well. I’m writing because .

Bullet 1 First of all, .

Template · Semi-formal email

Hi ,

My name is , and I live in / I work in .

Bullet 1 I wanted to let you know that .

Bullet 2 I’m afraid this means .

Bullet 3 Would it be possible to ? I’m happy to help with .

Phrases that score
Explain: Unfortunately, … · To make matters worse, … · As a result, …
Feelings: I was very disappointed … · This was extremely inconvenient …
Request: I would appreciate it if … · Would it be possible to …?
Close: Thank you for your understanding. · I look forward to your reply.
Model (formal, bullet 2)
As a result, I had to sit on the floor for two weeks while I waited for a replacement. This was extremely inconvenient because my parents were visiting, and we had nowhere comfortable to sit.
Each bullet paragraph = 2–3 sentences: a detail + a reason or example.
CELPIP Templates · Writing Task 2

The Survey Response

26 minutes · 150–200 words
Choose ONE option and defend it
Your 26 minutes
1 min choose · 3 min outline · 18 min write · 4 min proofread
How to choose
Pick the option with more and easier reasons, not the one you prefer.
Never
Never choose both. Never stay neutral. Two developed reasons beat five short ones.

Template · 5-part survey response

1 · Position In my opinion, Option , , is the better choice for (our city / our company / families).

2 · Reason 1 First, . For example, . As a result, .

3 · Reason 2 Another important reason is that . In my experience, .

4 · Concession Admittedly, Option would . However, .

5 · Conclusion For these reasons, I strongly believe that is the wiser choice.

Model · "A dog park (A) or a community vegetable garden (B)?"

In my opinion, Option B, a community vegetable garden, is the better choice for our neighbourhood. First, a garden brings neighbours together. For example, in my last city, families met every Saturday to plant and water, and many became close friends. As a result, the street felt safer and friendlier.

Another important reason is that a garden helps families save money. Fresh tomatoes and herbs are expensive, and growing them teaches children where food comes from.

Admittedly, a dog park would give pets a safe place to run. However, it would serve only dog owners, while a garden is open to everyone, including seniors and children.

For these reasons, I strongly believe that a community garden is the wiser use of the space.

Phrases that score
Position: In my view, … · I strongly support Option …
Reasons: First, … · Another key reason is … · Moreover, …
Examples: For example, … · In my experience, … · For instance, …
Concession: Admittedly, … However, … · Although …, …
Conclusion: For these reasons, … · Overall, …
Grow a thin reason
Reason → buses help people get to work
Example → my neighbour takes two buses for 80 minutes
Result → so she is often late and tired
Contrast → a direct route would cut her trip in half
Proofread: clear position · 2 reasons with examples · concession · 150–200 words.

Prep notes

Out of ideas?
